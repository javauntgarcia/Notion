Tuesday
Run + Mobility
Hip Flexors · Lower Back Run 25–35 min moderate + ~20 min stretch
▶
Run — Moderate Effort 25–35 min, conversational pace Legs already worked Monday — keep this aerobic, not a hard effort
Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch 2 × 60 s each side Tuck pelvis under, push hips forward, reach same-side arm overhead
Couch Stretch 2 × 60 s each side Back foot up on a couch or wall, front foot forward in a lunge
90/90 Hip Stretch 2 × 60 s each side Both legs at 90°, sit tall, lean forward over the front shin
Cat-Cow 2 × 10 slow reps Breathe with the movement — inhale to arch, exhale to round through the lower back
Child's Pose 2 × 60 s Arms extended, breathe into the lower back, let it decompress
Standing Quad Stretch 2 × 45 s each side Hold ankle, knees together, posterior tilt for a deeper hip flexor stretch too
Pelvic Floor — Kegels 2 × 10 reps, 5 s hold / 5 s release Do these lying down after the run, separate from the stretching flow
